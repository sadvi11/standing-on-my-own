Open with the concrete thing. A moment, a sentence someone said, a thing you
noticed. Not a definition.

The daily ones do not have to conclude. A note that ends on an open question is
honest; a note that manufactures a lesson is not, and readers can tell.

## kind: note or essay

`note` — short, daily, thinking out loud. Most of what you write.

`essay` — longer, worked out, the ones you would send someone.

Writing daily does not mean finishing daily. The split exists so it does not
have to.

## date

`YYYY-MM-DD`. The build refuses anything else, so a typo fails loudly instead
of sorting your archive wrongly.

## Before publishing

- Is there something concrete in the first three lines?
- Am I writing what I actually think, or what sounds wise?
- If someone I wrote about read this, would it be fair?
