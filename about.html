Replace all of this. It is scaffolding, not your voice.

Things worth covering, in whatever order sounds like you:

Who you are. Enough that a reader knows who is talking. You do not owe anyone your whole history.

Why this subject. The honest reason. Not "I want to help women" — the specific thing that made you start writing this down.

What you are not. You are not a therapist. Saying so once, plainly, protects you and sets the reader's expectations correctly.

What a reader gets. How often you write, and what they can expect from it.
